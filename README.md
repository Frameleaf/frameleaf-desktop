# Frameleaf Desktop

**Put your computer to work for your photos.**

Frameleaf Desktop for Mac, Windows and Linux opens your photo library in its own window and lends your computer's processing power to your server's face recognition, smart search and text recognition. It can also copy photo folders to your server, or host the whole library on the computer itself.

[**Download Frameleaf Desktop**](https://github.com/Frameleaf/frameleaf-desktop/releases/latest) · [Take the tour](https://frameleaf.app/apps/desktop/) · [Install guide](https://frameleaf.app/docs/apps/desktop/#install) · [Get Frameleaf](https://frameleaf.app/)

## Choose how you use it

- **Connect to my server**: sign in with Frameleaf Cloud, use Find My Server, or type your server's address. Your computer can then help with AI processing.
- **Run Frameleaf here**: host your whole library on this computer. A local Manager starts, stops, restarts and backs up the server, and the library keeps running after you close the window.

## Private and secure

- Your password is used once to sign in and never saved. The session and device key live in your system keychain or credential store.
- The Mac app is notarized, and every release publishes checksums.

## FAQ

**Does it replace my server?**
Only if you choose *Run Frameleaf here*.

**Will AI processing slow my computer down?**
It runs below your other apps, only when conditions are right, and you can pause it at any time.

**Is my password saved?**
No. It's used once to sign in; the session and device key are stored in your system keychain or credential store.
